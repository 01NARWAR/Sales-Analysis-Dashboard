# Business Understanding

## Project Name
Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Domain
Retail / Superstore Sales

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales performance using historical retail sales data and build an interactive dashboard that helps business users monitor KPIs, identify trends, improve profitability, and support data-driven decision making.

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate thousands of sales transactions every year. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify:

- Best-selling products
- Low-performing products
- High-profit and low-profit categories
- Sales trends over time
- Regional performance
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Return rate
- Discount impact on profit

Management needs an interactive dashboard to monitor business performance and make strategic decisions.

## Target Users

- Sales Manager
- Regional Manager
- Business Analyst
- Store Manager
- Senior Management

## Expected Business Outcome

The dashboard should help decision-makers:

- Increase sales
- Improve profit
- Reduce return rate
- Identify top-performing products
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Make data-driven business decisions
