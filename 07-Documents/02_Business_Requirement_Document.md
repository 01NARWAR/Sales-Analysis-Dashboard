# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## Project Name

Sales Analysis Dashboard

---

# Business Requirements

The business requires an interactive dashboard that provides complete visibility into sales performance across different regions, customers, products, and time periods.

The dashboard should enable management to quickly identify business opportunities and performance issues.

---

# Functional Requirements

The dashboard must provide:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Sales
- Average Profit
- Return Rate

---

# Analysis Required

## Sales Analysis

- Sales by Category
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by State
- Sales Trend (Monthly)
- Sales Trend (Yearly)

---

## Profit Analysis

- Profit by Category
- Profit by State
- Profit by Discount
- Loss Making Products

---

## Customer Analysis

- Top Customers
- Customer Segments
- Customer Contribution

---

## Product Analysis

- Best Selling Products
- Worst Selling Products
- Top 10 Products
- Bottom 10 Products

---

## Return Analysis

- Return Rate
- Returned Orders
- Returned Products

---

# Filters

The dashboard should support:

- Region
- State
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Segment
- Ship Mode
- Year

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it enables business users to:

- Monitor KPIs
- Identify trends
- Improve profitability
- Reduce return rate
- Make data-driven decisions
